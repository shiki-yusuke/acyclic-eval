# Code of Conduct

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate in
issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions. Harassment, personal attacks,
discrimination, doxxing, and publication of another person's sensitive data
are not acceptable.

When reporting a concern, do not disclose private transcripts, prompts,
credentials, or local logs in public. Use GitHub's private reporting features
where appropriate. Maintainers may remove content that violates these
expectations and take proportionate action to protect participants and the
project.
